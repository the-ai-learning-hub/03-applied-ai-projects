# 📂 my_media/ · your own photos and videos

Copy your own photos (`.jpg`, `.png`, `.webp`) and short videos (`.mp4`, `.mov`, `.avi`, `.mkv`, `.webm`) into this folder: every stage of the [Challenge 2 notebook](../Challenge_2_YOLO_Live.ipynb) picks them up automatically (re-run the setup cell after adding files). In Google Colab, use the 📤 upload cell of the notebook instead.

- Short videos work best (10–20 s): a laptop CPU processes about 20–40 frames per second.
- Everything in this folder except this README is ignored by git, so your files are not committed by accident.
- Do not film or photograph other people without their permission.
